STAGE 2

Ideal Best Friend Match

5 Must-Have Qualities

  • 1. Patient: Shaxriyor needs a patient friend because he sometimes makes impulsive decisions during experiments.
  • 2. Supportive: Encourages him during football tournaments and study sessions.
  • 3. Honest: Gives sincere feedback when reviewing homework or ideas.
  • 4. Active: Enjoys outdoor activities, sports, and team games.
  • 5. Open-minded: Willing to explore new scientific theories and games together.

2 Qualities to Avoid

  • 1. Dishonest: Trust is the foundation of Shaxriyor's ideal friendship.
  • 2. Insensitive: A good friend should care about feelings and maintain mutual respect.

Common Interests & Shared Activities:

They should share a mutual love for science experiments, biology, and football. Activities they enjoy doing together include training on the football field, conducting hands-on chemistry tests in the lab, and co-op computer gaming on weekends.
